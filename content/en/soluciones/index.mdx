# Solutions by type of organisation

The corporate work of a group with twelve subsidiaries looks nothing like that of a mutual society with a hundred thousand members, or that of a law firm acting as company secretary for eighty clients. Sttok is the same platform in every case, but what it solves changes.

## Family businesses

Shareholdings spread across generations, inheritances that fragment the shares, co-ownership, usufruct and restrictions on transfer in the bylaws.

[See the solution for family businesses](/en/solutions/family-business)

## Corporate groups

Family holding companies, operating parent companies and subsidiaries. One register per company, general meetings and boards per company, and the need to know who controls what at the end of the chain.

[See the solution for corporate groups](/en/solutions/corporate-groups)

## Entities with thousands of shareholders

Mutual societies, cooperatives and companies with a broad membership. Notices at scale, quorum, proxies and a register that changes every week.

[See the solution for entities with thousands of shareholders](/en/solutions/large-shareholder-bases)

## Board secretariat

Notice, attendance, proxies, voting, minutes and certification of board resolutions, with the register of officers and their terms.

[See the solution for the board secretariat](/en/solutions/board-secretariat)

## Companies

Corporate management for a company with several shareholders: register, general meetings, board and incentive plans. The tax treatment of those plans is covered in the [guide to phantom shares and stock options](/guias/fiscalidad-phantom-shares-stock-options) (in Spanish).

[See the solution for companies](/en/solutions/companies)

## Law firms and advisers

Corporate management of the whole client portfolio from a single login, with each company's documents in their place.

[See the solution for law firms](/en/solutions/lawyers)

## In-house legal teams

Registers, ownership certificates, corporate calendar, general meetings, boards and officers for every company an in-house legal team looks after.

[See the solution for in-house legal teams](/en/solutions/in-house-legal)

## Funds and investors

Tracking of investees, up-to-date positions and access to corporate documents without asking for them by email.

[See the solution for investors](/en/solutions/investors)

## Outside Spain

Companies in Portugal, Andorra, Colombia, Mexico or Chile, and groups with subsidiaries in several countries. The register supports the share numbering, company types and body names of each jurisdiction.

[See jurisdictions (in Spanish)](/jurisdicciones)
